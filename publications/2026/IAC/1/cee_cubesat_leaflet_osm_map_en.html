Regional value is inherently cross-border

A CubeSat is rarely attributable to one country or company. Mauve combines a UK owner and data-service model with Hungarian C3S platform and integration capability. CroCube is Croatia’s national mission, with Slovak integration and Hungarian operations links. The map therefore treats missions as regional value networks.

“Evidence gap” does not mean poor mission performance. It means that a financial, workforce or sustainability indicator was not publicly available, could not be assigned to one mission, or was not comparable across cases. Planned-mission outcomes are not counted as realised returns.
